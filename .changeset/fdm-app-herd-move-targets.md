Only offer herds an animal can move to. The "Doelkoppel" list on the animal page and in "Dieren verplaatsen" on a herd card shows only herds of the same category or of a next life stage that the sex of the animals allows. The form says when animals of unknown sex get a sex by the move, and when there is no herd to move to. A rejected move shows a Dutch warning, and the "Melkvee" chapter of the manual explains the rules.
