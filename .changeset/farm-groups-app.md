Add farm groups for organizations. Organization farms in the farm overview are shown under collapsible group headers when groups exist. The organization farms table gets a "Groepen" column, a group filter and a bulk action to assign farms to groups. The organization balance pages (nitrogen, organic matter), indicators, measures and the atlas indicators map get a group picker that selects the farms of a group. Groups are managed on the new `/organization/:slug/groups` page.
