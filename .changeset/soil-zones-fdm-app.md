Soil analyses read from a PDF now keep where the field was sampled: the locations of the subsamples and the zone the analysis represents. The detail page of a soil analysis, also in the atlas, shows a map with the field boundary, the sampled zone and the sample points. When a soil value combines several zones of a large field, the soil cards show "Gemiddelde van N zones" with the value per zone, and warn when the zones cover too little of the field.
