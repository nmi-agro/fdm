Soil analyses read from a PDF now keep where the field was sampled: the locations of the subsamples and the zone the analysis represents. The detail page of a soil analysis, also in the atlas, shows a map with the field boundary, the sampled zone and the sample points. When a soil value combines several zones of a large field, the soil cards show "Gemiddelde van N zones" with the value per zone, and show next to the value how much of the field the zones cover when that is too little. The soil page of a field shows a map of all soil analyses, each in its own colour that matches the list, with a legend; the list says for each analysis whether it applies to the whole field or to a zone, and which share of the field. Pointing at or clicking the coverage of a value highlights the zones behind it on that map; elsewhere it opens the soil analysis with its sampling map. The soil analysis atlas can show the sampling zones, coloured by their own value, and the sample points of the selected parameter with the new "Toon bemonstering" toggle; hovering a zone or point shows the analysis it belongs to. The soil analysis atlas now uses the same field style as the fertilization advice atlas: a white dotted outline and semi-transparent fills.

The calculated NLV is shown as one field-level value instead of a value per zone, and its coverage reflects soil organic matter, clay and C/N together. Sampling geometries read from a PDF keep only longitude and latitude (also when the report adds a height), and empty or invalid geometries are skipped instead of blocking the upload. The sampling map of a soil analysis clips the zone to the field before deciding whether it covers the whole field.
