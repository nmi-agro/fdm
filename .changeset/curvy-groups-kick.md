fdm-helpdesk can now track attachments added to any message. Users can add and remove attachments on their own messages, for which they are authorized based on whether they have write access to the message. They can view attachments across the helpdesk as long as they can view the message each attachments is attached to. fdm-helpdesk does not deal with or care about attachment data storage or email notifications.
