Add `assessPhosphateStatus` (also in `@nmi-agro/fdm-calculator/browser`): judges a field's soil phosphorus against a target zone (P-AL at least 35, PSD at most the field threshold, P-AL 35–45 when the PSD is not usable) from the long-term average balance. It returns the aim (`opbouwen` / `onderhouden` / `afbouwen`), the bound that sets it, a `conflict` flag when the environment requires reducing while P-AL is not high, the direction, a verdict (`good` / `attention` / `adjust` / `unknown`), the years with their range, and the long-term balance needed to reach or stay in the zone, with the fertilizer supply it implies. The rules are in `PHOSPHATE_STATUS_RULES`. The farm result has `status_area` (area and number of fields per verdict), `long_term` has the average `fertilizers` supply, and `yearsTo` and `kgP2O5PerPsdPoint` are exported. Buffer strips, nature and landscape elements are no longer calculated in `calculatePhosphateBalance`: their field result gets `exclusion` (no balance, no error) and they are left out of the farm average; `getPhosphateFieldExclusion` applies the same rule as the mineralisation. `PhosphateSaturation` has `a_p_al_source`, the source of the soil analysis P-AL was taken from, next to `analysis_sources` (the sources of the PSD).
