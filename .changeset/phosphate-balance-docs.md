Document the phosphate balance (`insights/balance/03-phosphate-balance.md`): inputs, supply (fertilizers, mineralisation in two tracks, deposition), removal, leaching, phosphorus saturation degree and field threshold, the illustrative projection with year classes, the farm and organisation averages and the output. The harvest docs explain where the phosphate content of a harvest comes from. The app gets an optional `PUBLIC_FDM_DOCS_URL` (default `https://nmi-agro.github.io/fdm`), and the phosphate help dialog links to the new docs page.
