Document the phosphate balance (`insights/balance/03-phosphate-balance.md`): inputs, supply (fertilizers, mineralisation in two tracks, deposition), removal, leaching, phosphorus saturation degree and field threshold, the illustrative projection with year classes, the farm and organisation averages and the output. The harvest docs explain where the phosphate content of a harvest comes from. In the app, the phosphate pages link to the "Fosfaatbalans" section of the user guide, like the other balances: the help dialog, a help link next to the title and the "Handleiding" item in the sidebar. The section shows a screenshot of the farm page. A new background page "Fosfaatbalans" in the user guide explains the current calculation rules (balance, long-term average, phosphorus saturation and environmental threshold, projection and verdicts); the help dialog links to it.
