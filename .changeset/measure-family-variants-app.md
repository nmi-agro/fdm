Recommended measures now show at most one variant per measure family (for example only the strongest of "Hoger stuwpeil in hoofdwatergang" 20 cm and 35 cm), so the freed slot goes to another measure. This applies to "Aanbevolen maatregelen" on the farm measures overview, "Waar te beginnen" in the Knelpunten panel and the recommended measures per indicator on the field page. A variant is no longer recommended for a field that already has another variant of the same family.
