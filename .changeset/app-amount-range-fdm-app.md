The fertilizer application form now rejects an amount above 100,000 kg/ha and shows the maximum in the unit of the selected fertilizer, for example "Hoeveelheid mag niet groter zijn dan 95,23 m³/ha". When an amount is rejected while saving, for example in the bulk edit, a clear warning is shown instead of a general error. Moving a fertilizer application in the timeline keeps its stored amount.
