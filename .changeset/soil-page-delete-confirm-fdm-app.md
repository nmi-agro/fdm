Deleting a soil analysis on the soil page of a field now asks for confirmation first, names the analysis and says when its PDF is deleted too. The soil cards are easier to read and to use with a keyboard or screen reader: small text is at least 12px, parameter names wrap instead of being cut off, values line up within a row, and the descriptions and zone details open on keyboard focus. The colour for destructive actions is a darker red, so that white text on red buttons and red text meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio.
