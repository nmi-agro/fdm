Redesign the Mineralisatie app (Labs) around Dyna v2. The farm overview shows how many fields are calculated, a sortable table with per field the available nitrogen, uptake and leaching, and the fields that need attention: an expected nitrogen shortage, rain around a planned application or harvest, a nitrogen surplus or a failed calculation. Fields without a harvest are grouped per crop and link to the harvest form of the crop rotation, where the harvests or cuts of all those fields can be entered at once; that form now returns to the page that opened it. The field page shows an interactive chart of nitrogen supply, uptake, available nitrogen and nitrate leaching with sowing, fertilization and harvest events, the weather forecast, the workable nitrogen balance, the data used for the calculation, and a what-if scenario to shift or change applications and harvests; for grassland every cut of the year is shown and cuts can be changed, added or removed. Each harvest shows the same fields as the harvest form for its crop (for grassland the yield and crude protein); a new cut starts with the usual values for the crop, and saving stores the harvests with these values. Amounts below 1 can be typed in the scenario. The effect of a scenario is shown next to the chart and on the event lane, and before saving the page lists what will change in the fertilization plan. Planning, growing season and evaluation are pre-selected based on the date and can be switched. The order of the page follows the phase: when planning, the scenario sits right under the chart; in the growing season the nitrogen balance and attention points come first, followed by the weather and the scenario. When the harvest is missing, the page asks for the measured or planned harvest, with the same fields as the harvest form for the crop, filled in with its usual values; they can also be left empty to save only the date. The pages for soil nitrogen supply (MINIP, PMN, Century) are removed. Calculations stream in per field, so the page no longer waits for all fields.

Return links after saving (for example on the fertilizer application, fertilizer and harvest forms) are now resolved against the app's own address, so a crafted link can no longer send users to another site.

The weather forecast is shown in the growing season only. Harvests or cuts of one cultivation need at least 10 days between them for the calculation: a harvest closer to the previous one is left out, and the page and farm overview warn which ones were not included. In a scenario, any number of cuts can be added, as long as they are at least 10 days apart.

A note under the page title says the Mineralisatie pages are experimental in design and model, available to test users only, and links to Ondersteuning for feedback.
