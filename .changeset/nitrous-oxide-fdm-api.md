Describe the nitrous oxide (N₂O) emission in the OpenAPI schema of the nitrogen balance of a field, a farm and a group of farms: `emission.nitrous_oxide`, in kg N/ha, with its CO₂-equivalent. The N₂O emission is now part of the balance and of the total emission, so the balance and the nitrate leaching become lower.
