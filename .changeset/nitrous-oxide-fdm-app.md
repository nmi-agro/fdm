Show the nitrous oxide (N₂O) emission on the nitrogen balance of a farm and a field: a card "Lachgasemissie" in kg N/ha with the CO₂-equivalent next to the card "Nitraatuitspoeling", both marked as not part of the balance, an N₂O bar in the balance chart, and "Lachgas" under the emissions in "Posten" with the emission from fertilizers, crop residues, soil and indirect emission. The balance chart now also stays readable on phones, and the balance pages use Dutch number formatting. The impact preview of the bulk fertilizer page keeps the RVO fertilizer type of a newly added fertilizer. The manual explains how the N₂O emission is calculated.
