Show the nitrous oxide (N₂O) emission on the nitrogen balance of an organization, a farm and a field: a card "Lachgasemissie" in kg N/ha with the CO₂-equivalent next to the card "Nitraatuitspoeling", an N₂O bar in the balance chart, and "Lachgas" under the emissions in "Posten" with the emission from fertilizers, crop residues and soil. The N₂O emission now counts in the balance; the nitrate leaching card is marked as not part of the balance. The balance chart now also stays readable on phones, and the balance pages use Dutch number formatting, without a minus sign for values that round to zero. The impact preview of the bulk fertilizer page keeps the RVO fertilizer type of a newly added fertilizer. The manual explains how the N₂O emission is calculated.
