Add grazing derivation engine with constants from the _Handboek Melkveehouderij_, computing distinct weidedagen, weide-uren, pooled Weidemelk 120x6 compliance, beweidingsplatform, stocking densities and rest periods. The beweidingsplatform uses the grazed area per field (`b_area_grazed`), so successive grazing strips on a field add up. Rest periods are counted per part of a field (`calculateRestBeforeUses`), so successive strips each get their own rest. The rest is reported as information without a guideline or alert: whether enough grass has grown depends on growth and grazing, which are not modelled. The rest is in growth days, from the day after a use up to the day before the next (Handboek §3.9.2: end 4 May, start 26 May: 21 days), whatever time of day the end was stored with. Every grazing input has an end (`l_grazing_end` is required); its days up to today are realised and the later days planned. Uses of the same part that overlap or meet on one day, and grazings on successive days (omweiden recorded day by day), count as one grazing period, also in a chain such as 1–5, 3–8 and 6–10 May; the rest counts from the start of the period. The period of a use is built only from uses of its own part, so a grazing of the whole field does not link two strips that do not overlap each other.

Weide-uren add up the hours of all grazings of a herd on one day (8 hours on one field or zone and 7 on another make 15), up to 24 hours; a Weidemelk day needs one dairy herd with at least 6 hours that day. The farm's weide-uren of a day are those of the herd that grazed longest, and the farm average divides them by the distinct days with recorded hours. `RestPeriodDetail.lastUseDate` is documented as the calendar day on which the previous use ended.

Add grazing allocation rules: `calculateGrazingAllocationArea` (area to allocate to a herd for a strip or plot, with the grass on offer at in-grazing plus the regrowth during grazing), `estimateGrassIntake` (grass intake and supplementation from the grass growth on the platform, following Nieuw Nederlands Weiden) and `calculatePlatformArea`, with reference values for grass growth, regrowth during grazing, sward heights and grazing hours. The grazing systems include Nieuw Nederlands Weiden: a new plot of equal size every day, with `NNW_PLOT_AREA_HA_PER_COW` (2,5 ha per 120 cows) and `NNW_DAY_AND_NIGHT_HOURS` (a new plot in the morning and evening above 12 grazing hours). Values that are not stated in the cited sources are documented as assumptions.
