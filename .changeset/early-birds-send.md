Add grazing derivation engine with constants from the _Handboek Melkveehouderij_, computing distinct weidedagen, weide-uren, pooled Weidemelk 120x6 compliance, beweidingsplatform, stocking densities, rest periods, and overgrazing alerts. The beweidingsplatform uses the grazed area per field (`b_area_grazed`), so successive grazing strips on a field add up. Rest periods are counted per part of a field (`calculateRestBeforeUses`), so successive strips are not flagged as overgrazing; the rest threshold is 21 days (Handboek table 3.8). The rest is the difference between the end date of a use and the start date of the next (end 4 May, start 25 May: 21 days), whatever time of day the end was stored with, and a grazing without an end that has started counts through today. Uses of the same part that run at the same time, or on the same day, count as one use, so two herds on one zone get no rest of 0 days.

Weide-uren add up the hours of all grazings of a herd on one day (8 hours on one field or zone and 7 on another make 15), up to 24 hours; a Weidemelk day needs one dairy herd with at least 6 hours that day. The farm's weide-uren of a day are those of the herd that grazed longest, and the farm average divides them by the distinct days with recorded hours. `RestPeriodDetail.lastUseDate` is documented as the calendar day on which the previous use ended.

Add grazing allocation rules: `calculateGrazingAllocationArea` (area to allocate to a herd for a strip or plot, with the grass on offer at in-grazing plus the regrowth during grazing), `estimateGrassIntake` (grass intake and supplementation from the grass growth on the platform, following Nieuw Nederlands Weiden) and `calculatePlatformArea`, with reference values for grass growth, regrowth during grazing, sward heights and grazing hours. The grazing systems include Nieuw Nederlands Weiden: a new plot of equal size every day, with `NNW_PLOT_AREA_HA_PER_COW` (2,5 ha per 120 cows) and `NNW_DAY_AND_NIGHT_HOURS` (a new plot in the morning and evening above 12 grazing hours). Values that are not stated in the cited sources are documented as assumptions.
