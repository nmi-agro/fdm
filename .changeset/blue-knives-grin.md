Add dairy grazing and livestock interfaces:

- **Melkvee overview** (`/livestock`): key figures (animals and GVE, grazing days, Weidemelk progress, grazing platform with its GVE/ha), the grazing of the last and the coming week per field with the planned grazing, and the herd cards with their count steppers (a count that was changed elsewhere in the meantime is not applied; the page asks to reload). A herd card shows "Loopt nu op …" for the whole last day of a grazing, also for a grazing of one day. Outside the grazing season the overview says so instead of showing an empty week. Add a grazing, an animal or a herd straight from the page. Also add a first-herd form (`/livestock/new`), a Vee page (`/livestock/animals`) with first the herds and their actions (count, rename, move animals, remove, add a herd) and then all animals, sorted by work number and searchable on every column and an animal page (`/livestock/animal/:l_id_animal`) with herd reassignment and departure. In "Dier toevoegen" and on the animal page, a Dutch levensnummer of a cattle animal fills in the werknummer (the 4 digits before the last digit), unless a werknummer of your own was typed; an invalid levensnummer is pointed out before saving, and an empty levensnummer removes it.
- **Beweidingskalender** (`/livestock/grazing`, with the planner and insights under it): Add a calendar of the grassland fields with every day of the grazing season (1 March to 31 October), scrolling sideways on smaller screens. Grazing is green, a planned grazing hatched, mowing dark green, and a fertilizer application is a dot in the colour of its fertilizer badge (brown for manure, blue for mineral fertilizer). The Atlas layer, the field grazing page and the overview use the same colours. Grazed fields come first, then the largest fields. A click on an empty day adds a grazing, dragging adds a longer period, and a click on a day with a grazing, mowing or fertilizer application opens it; when a day has more than one, a short list asks which one to open. Hovering or focusing a day shows its date, field and use in a tooltip. With the keyboard, Tab (or "ga naar vandaag in de kalender") puts the focus on today, the arrow keys move between days and fields, Enter opens a day and Shift with an arrow selects several days. A right click offers to open what is recorded on that day, or to add a grazing, a mowing or a fertilizer application there. Mowing and fertilizing use the forms of the Tijdlijn in a dialog; the toolbar above the calendar has "Beweiding toevoegen", "Meer toevoegen" (mowing and fertilizing), the planner and the map, and "Uitleg" explains the calendar. A field used again before the rest guideline shows as one short notice that links to Weidegang.
- **Grazing dialog**: One dialog adds, changes and removes a grazing everywhere: from a calendar cell, "Beweiding toevoegen" on the calendar and the Atlas layer, and the field's grazing page, where every grazing of the year opens, also one outside the season of the calendar. It first asks for "1 dag" (one date, which is both the first and the last day) or "Meerdere dagen" (a first and last day); every grazing has a last day. It starts at "Beperkt (8u)". It says when a date falls outside the calendar, that the first grazing of a year turns grazing on for the stikstofgebruiksnorm, and why "Vastleggen" waits for a zone. Changing a grazing keeps hours that were never recorded unknown and keeps a stored partial area as it is, unless the user changes them; a date typed in and confirmed with Enter is saved as shown. Removing a grazing asks for confirmation. A herd can have more than one grazing on a field on a day (another zone, or the same zone again), as long as its grazings add up to at most 24 hours that day; otherwise the dialog says so. A typed date such as 2026-05-01 is the day shown, also in a browser west of UTC.
- **Planner & Insights**: Add a Beweidingsplanner for the grazing systems (graslandgebruikssystemen, including Standweiden, Modern standweiden and Nieuw Nederlands Weiden); a plan for an earlier year records grazing that already happened, and once this year's season is over the planner points to next year. For Nieuw Nederlands Weiden the planner divides the chosen fields into plots of equal size ("Perceelsgrootte") and gives the herd a new plot every day, or every half day when it grazes day and night; each plot is its own grazing with its share of the hours. The plan runs up to and including its last day, a partial area is not rounded down to 0 ha, and a period the herd cannot graze (more than 24 hours on a day with its recorded grazing, a day before the herd started or after it ended, or a day without a grass cultivation on the field) is skipped with the reason. Add a Weidegang KPI dashboard, field grazing subpage, and Tijdlijn grazing lanes. The key figures of a past year count the herds of that year, also herds that have ended since, a grazing that started in the previous year counts its days in the selected year, the grazing hours of a herd on one day add up over its grazings, and zones count only within their field for the rest periods. Weidegang says "Niet gehaald" only once 120 days can no longer be reached in the year, and the month bars follow the number of days in each month. The calendar hatches the days after today of a grazing that has started as planned, marks today as the server does, and "Gepland deze week" on the overview lists only grazings that have not started. The field's grazing map shows only zones grazed so far. Only grass harvests count as mowing, and "today" is the calendar day in the Netherlands, also for the centre of the overview and the default day of new entries. The planner waits for a valid period, start date and plot size before it makes a plan.
- **Sidebar**: "Melkvee" opens the overview and expands to "Beweidingskalender", "Beweidingsplanner", "Weidegang" and "Vee" (the list of all animals); it is expanded while a Melkvee page is open. Without a herd the sidebar offers "Melkvee toevoegen".
- **Atlas layer "Beweiding"**: Show per day which fields are grazed (with a dark outline), mown, resting or not yet used in the selected year, with the grazed zones, a legend with the number of fields per status and a popup per field. Pick the day with the arrows or a date picker; the layer opens on today's day and month in the selected year. Add a grazing for the shown day from the panel. The layer is greyed out until the farm has a herd.
- **Farm page**: Add a "Beweiding vandaag" action that opens the Beweiding layer for the current year, for farms with a herd. Under "Overzichten", a "Beweidingskalender" card opens the calendar, for farms with a herd.
- **Grazing zones**: Record which part of a field was grazed: the whole field, a zone drawn on the map, or a percentage without a location. While drawing, points snap to the corners and edges of the field, of recent strips and of the current zone, with a visible snap point (hold Alt to draw freely), and a finished zone is clipped to the field with a message when part was cut off. Drawing again replaces the zone, "Ongedaan maken" (or Ctrl+Z) undoes the last change or corner, Escape stops a drawing without closing the dialog, and "Aanpassen" selects the zone at once. Earlier zones show their date, herd and use count and are highlighted on the map on hover; the map has "Perceel in beeld" and a legend. Reuse zones from earlier grazings on the field, or let the strip builder cut a strip of the right size for the herd (grass intake × days ÷ grass on offer) next to the strips of the 28 days before the grazing, counted per grazing so a later use of the same zone does not hide an earlier one. When the earlier zones could not be loaded (or there are too many to take into account), the strip builder waits and "Opnieuw laden" tries again; while they are loaded for another date it waits too. Its herd size and grass intake follow the dialog until the user types a value, and its inputs never block saving the grazing; a zone that the map cannot fully load is shown but not edited, so no part of it is lost. The planner can generate successive strips on the map, the field grazing page and the Beweiding layer show the zones, and the grazing platform counts all strips on a field together.
- **Phones and tablets**: The Melkvee pages fit a phone screen. Their breadcrumbs show only the farm and the current page below 1280 px, like the other pages. The Weidegang key figures and the field's grazing figures show two by two, the planner puts the period dates below each other and stacks its buttons, and the calendar pins a narrower field column. On every page a row of title buttons now wraps instead of running off the screen, and a long "Terug naar …" button moves to its own line below the breadcrumb.

Third review round: the strip builder counts a grazing of the whole field in the 28 days before as occupied and says the free part is unknown after a grazing with only an area; it also finds grazings that started before that window. The field page shows the stored days in any time zone, counts the rest at the start of a year from a use in the year before, and only changes or removes a grazing of its own field. The calendar keeps the keyboard focus on its field when the fields are reordered. The planner checks the hours (above 0, at most 24), a period of at most 60 days and dates within the chosen year, with messages linked to their inputs; the fields are checkboxes; it starts again for another farm or year, uses the herds of the plan year and sizes a shorter last strip for its own days. The overview takes the grassland area and today from the calendar, and a reader without write access sees no add-herd action, also not in the sidebar. The Melkvee menu shows for a farm that has had a herd. The animal page only opens an animal of the farm, counts each realised grazing day once within the animal's time in the herd, keeps an animal with a future departure in its herd, and keeps the birth date as a calendar day.

Fourth review round: every grazing is on a field, so the planner no longer checks for grazings without a field; it uses every herd that ran during the plan year and skips days before the herd started or after it ended, and days on which a field has no grass cultivation, with the reason, and a herd that disappears after a reload has to be chosen again. Mowing and fertilizer dates count on their calendar day in the Netherlands in the calendar, Weidegang, the Beweiding layer and the field page. The Beweiding layer counts only grass harvests as mowing, uses the fields of the selected year, opens on the server's today and reads only the herds and grassland fields for the dialog. The calendar opens the current year instead of all years, reads no grazing data for a farm without a herd, and offers "Melkvee toevoegen" only with write access; Weidegang without a herd goes to the Melkvee overview. In the calendar a long press opens the menu of the pressed day, "ga naar vandaag in de kalender" focuses today, leaving the calendar ends a Shift selection, a selection shows on days with a use, a drag only completes on a day of the same field, and the day chooser shows the hours, area and period of a grazing. The average grazing hours read "Gemiddeld per weidedag met vastgelegde uren". In the grazing dialog the hours and the percentage take any value above 0, a stored part keeps one decimal, and a new grazing on the field page starts on today's day in the selected year. The strip builder only waits when the land in use is unknown, still loading, or belongs to another date, and asks for a start date first; "Opnieuw laden" also shows when only the earlier zones failed. On the animal page a departure today is recorded now and one on another day at the end of that day, a cleared birth date is removed, and the days of the year and of a herd move follow the Dutch calendar. The herd cards and "Beweiding vandaag" on the farm page take the day from the server. A grazing without an end on the field page counts in its start year only. The Melkvee pages no longer add their own main landmark inside the farm shell.

Layout: on wide screens the Beweidingsplanner shows the plan and map next to the steps, "Melkvee toevoegen" shows what it creates next to the form, Weidegang puts Weidemelk next to the points of attention, and the field's grazing page shows the zones map next to its history, with a planned grazing marked "Gepland". Decorative dots, icons and tints are gone from the herd cards, the calendar's herd filter, Weidegang and the planner, and the card footers on the animal page have space above their line. The departure date on the animal page starts empty, or at a planned departure, which the page now shows.

A new herd ("Melkvee toevoegen" and the "Koppel toevoegen" dialog) asks "Aanwezig sinds": the day its animals arrived and joined the herd, by default 1 January of the selected year (or today for a year still to come), as the real day is often unknown. A day in the future is refused. The plus on a herd card now opens "Dieren toevoegen aan …" with the number of animals and "Aanwezig sinds", by default today, so animals that were already there earlier count from that day. The overview no longer repeats how the counts are derived below the herd cards.

Design review: "Melkvee toevoegen" starts without a number of animals and marks the herd name as required; "Afvoer vastleggen" sits in the card footer and waits for a date; herd cards keep the category on one line and their footers aligned; Weidegang uses the same compact key figures as the overview; the Weidemelk label reads "Weidemelk (120 dagen × 6 uur)" everywhere; the calendar's herd choice reads "Vastleggen voor:" and a field in use shows "in gebruik" instead of "0 dagen rust"; the strip builder keeps two columns in the narrow dialog; on a 1920 px screen the planner's sticky plan keeps its button in view; the field page's map is narrower at 1280 px and a grazing that can be opened is underlined; the month bars and "Gepland deze week" sit closer to their content.
