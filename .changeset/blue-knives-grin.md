Add dairy grazing and livestock interfaces:

- **Melkvee overview** (`/livestock`): key figures (animals and GVE, grazing days, Weidemelk progress, grazing platform with its GVE/ha), the grazing of the last and the coming week per field with the planned grazing, and the herd cards with their count steppers. Outside the grazing season the overview says so instead of showing an empty week. Add a grazing, an animal or a herd straight from the page. Also add a first-herd form (`/livestock/new`), a Vee page (`/livestock/animals`) with first the herds and their actions (count, rename, move animals, remove, add a herd) and then all animals, sorted by work number and searchable on every column and an animal page (`/livestock/animal/:l_id_animal`) with herd reassignment and departure. In "Dier toevoegen" and on the animal page, a Dutch levensnummer of a cattle animal fills in the werknummer (the 4 digits before the last digit), unless a werknummer of your own was typed; an invalid levensnummer is pointed out before saving, and an empty levensnummer removes it.
- **Beweidingskalender** (`/livestock/grazing`, with the planner and insights under it): Add a calendar of the grassland fields with every day of the grazing season (1 March to 31 October), scrolling sideways on smaller screens. Grazing is green, a planned grazing hatched, mowing dark green, and a fertilizer application is a dot in the colour of its fertilizer badge (brown for manure, blue for mineral fertilizer). The Atlas layer, the field grazing page and the overview use the same colours. Grazed fields come first, then the largest fields. A click on an empty day adds a grazing, dragging adds a longer period, and a click on a day with a grazing, mowing or fertilizer application opens it. Hovering or focusing a day shows its date, field and use in a tooltip. With the keyboard, Tab (or "ga naar vandaag in de kalender") puts the focus on today, the arrow keys move between days and fields, Enter opens a day and Shift with an arrow selects several days. A right click offers to add a grazing, a mowing or a fertilizer application on that day. Mowing and fertilizing use the forms of the Tijdlijn in a dialog; the toolbar above the calendar has "Beweiding toevoegen", "Meer toevoegen" (mowing and fertilizing), the planner and the map, and "Uitleg" explains the calendar. A field used again before the rest guideline shows as one short notice that links to Weidegang.
- **Grazing dialog**: One dialog adds, changes and removes a grazing everywhere: from a calendar cell, "Beweiding toevoegen" on the calendar and the Atlas layer, and the field's grazing page, where every grazing of the year opens, also one outside the season of the calendar. It first asks for "1 dag" (one date) or "Meerdere dagen" (a first and last day), and starts at "Beperkt (8u)". It says when a date falls outside the calendar, that recording a grazing turns grazing on for the stikstofgebruiksnorm, and why "Vastleggen" waits for a zone. Removing a grazing asks for confirmation. When the herd already grazes the field on one of those days, the dialog explains that a herd can be on a field only once a day.
- **Planner & Insights**: Add a Beweidingsplanner for the grazing systems (graslandgebruikssystemen, including Standweiden, Modern standweiden and Nieuw Nederlands Weiden); a plan for an earlier year records grazing that already happened, and once this year's season is over the planner points to next year. For Nieuw Nederlands Weiden the planner divides the chosen fields into plots of equal size ("Perceelsgrootte") and gives the herd a new plot every day, or every half day when it grazes day and night; two plots of one day on the same field form one grazing. Add a Weidegang KPI dashboard, field grazing subpage, and Tijdlijn grazing lanes.
- **Sidebar**: "Melkvee" opens the overview and expands to "Beweidingskalender", "Beweidingsplanner", "Weidegang" and "Vee" (the list of all animals); it is expanded while a Melkvee page is open. Without a herd the sidebar offers "Melkvee toevoegen".
- **Atlas layer "Beweiding"**: Show per day which fields are grazed (with a dark outline), mown, resting or not yet used in the selected year, with the grazed zones, a legend with the number of fields per status and a popup per field. Pick the day with the arrows or a date picker; the layer opens on today's day and month in the selected year. Add a grazing for the shown day from the panel. The layer is greyed out until the farm has a herd.
- **Farm page**: Add a "Beweiding vandaag" action that opens the Beweiding layer for the current year, for farms with a herd.
- **Grazing zones**: Record which part of a field was grazed: the whole field, a zone drawn on the map, or a percentage without a location. While drawing, points snap to the corners and edges of the field, of recent strips and of the current zone, with a visible snap point (hold Alt to draw freely), and a finished zone is clipped to the field with a message when part was cut off. Drawing again replaces the zone, "Ongedaan maken" (or Ctrl+Z) undoes the last change or corner, Escape stops a drawing without closing the dialog, and "Aanpassen" selects the zone at once. Earlier zones show their date, herd and use count and are highlighted on the map on hover; the map has "Perceel in beeld" and a legend. Reuse zones from earlier grazings on the field, or let the strip builder cut a strip of the right size for the herd (grass intake × days ÷ grass on offer) next to the previous strips. The planner can generate successive strips on the map, the field grazing page and the Beweiding layer show the zones, and the grazing platform counts all strips on a field together.
