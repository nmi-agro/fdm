Phosphate balance pages are read as "where, which way, how long". Each field gets a verdict (Goed / Aandacht / Bijsturen) against a target zone: P-AL at least 25 for the yield, and the PSD at most its environmental threshold. Being close to a bound counts as reached. The field page opens with a status card that shows the aim (opbouwen / onderhouden / afbouwen, and whether the environment or the yield sets it), an advice with the needed long-term average saldo, and the position, direction and years as a band. When that saldo needs more fertilizer than the field's fosfaatgebruiksnorm, a warning says that the norm is lower and applies at farm level. The advice no longer asks to lower a fertilisation that is not there. The PSD and P-AL cards mark the bound that decides, P-AL shows its uncertainty range, and the long-term average saldo comes before the saldo of the year. The farm page starts with the area per verdict and lists the fields most urgent first. The field dashboard tile and the organisation page use the same verdicts, and the help dialog and docs explain the zone. Buffer strips, nature and landscape elements are left out of the phosphate pages: the farm page names them under the field list, the field page and the dashboard tile say why there is no balance, and they are not offered in the field picker. The PSD card says it is about the environment and the P-AL card that it is about the crop, also on the chart lines. Hovering a measured P-AL point shows its sampling date and laboratory, and the balance chart keeps two bars when the supply or the removal is zero. The "indicatief" note now says which values are estimated: a PSD from NMI BodemSchat estimates no longer reads as if P-AL was estimated too, and the P-AL details show the source of P-AL. The PSD chart shades the area below the milieugrens green, the trend lines have no hover dots, and the loading skeletons follow the layout of the phosphate farm, field and organisation pages.
