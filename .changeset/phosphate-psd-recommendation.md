Phosphate balance pages are read as "where, which way, how long". Each field gets a verdict (Goed / Aandacht / Bijsturen) against a target zone: P-AL at least 35 for the yield, and the PSD at most its environmental threshold. Being close to a bound counts as reached. The field page opens with a status card that shows the aim (opbouwen / onderhouden / afbouwen, and whether the environment or the yield sets it), an advice with the needed long-term average saldo, and the position, direction and years as a band. When that saldo needs more fertilizer than the field's fosfaatgebruiksnorm, a warning says that the norm is lower and applies at farm level. The advice no longer asks to lower a fertilisation that is not there. The PSD and P-AL cards mark the bound that decides, P-AL shows its uncertainty range, and the long-term average saldo comes before the saldo of the year. The farm page starts with the area per verdict and lists the fields most urgent first. The field dashboard tile and the organisation page use the same verdicts, and the help dialog and docs explain the zone. Buffer strips, nature and landscape elements are left out of the phosphate pages: the farm page names them under the field list, the field page and the dashboard tile say why there is no balance, and they are not offered in the field picker. The PSD card says it is about the environment and the P-AL card that it is about the crop, also on the chart lines. Hovering a measured P-AL point shows its sampling date and laboratory, and the balance chart keeps two bars when the supply or the removal is zero. The "indicatief" note now says which values are estimated: a PSD from NMI BodemSchat estimates no longer reads as if P-AL was estimated too, and the P-AL details show the source of P-AL. The PSD chart shades the area below the milieugrens green, the trend lines have no hover dots, and the loading skeletons follow the layout of the phosphate farm, field and organisation pages. After a design review: the status card leads with the target zone as one strip and three facts (position relative to the bounds, direction, how long), and the advice is a separate card ("Mogelijke aanpak") below the PSD and P-AL cards: the goal, the environmental (PSD) and agronomic (P-AL) view apart, and a short list of options phrased as possibilities, in kg P₂O₅/ha from fertilisers per year (with the saldo as secondary figure, and the time without phosphate fertilisation when stopping is not enough); direction is labelled against the aim; verdict badges are quieter, meet AA contrast, and explain themselves on focus or tap; PSD and P-AL charts share one time axis, show a streefzone and round ticks; the farm page has a sortable, filterable field table and groups the excluded fields; errors offer a retry and a short code; "Prototype" is now "Experimenteel" with a link to send feedback via Ondersteuning. The pages adapt to the available width: below about 56rem of card width the field table becomes a compact list with a sort menu, the status card and soil cards stack, chart ticks thin out instead of overlapping, the measured points open their tooltip on tap, and touch screens get larger targets. When the PSD is above the environmental limit while P-AL is below the lower bound, the target zone cannot be reached: the pages do not choose a goal but show the effect of the balance on both indicators, and of a lower or a higher balance.
