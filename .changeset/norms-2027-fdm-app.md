Show the usage norms (gebruiksruimte) and use Gerrit for 2027. The 2027 norms are provisional: a copy of the 2026 norms until RVO publishes the 2027 tables. A field's norms for 2027 no longer fall back to the 2025 rules, and Gerrit no longer falls back to 2025 for an unsupported year.

A plan that Gerrit generated is discarded when you switch to another year or farm, so it can no longer be accepted for a year it was not made for. Before, accepting such a plan replaced the fertilizer applications of the new year with those of the plan.
