Add a prototype phosphate balance page ("Fosfaat") under Balans, for the farm and per field. The farm page shows the balance, supply and removal in kg P₂O₅/ha and the status of each field. The field page adds the P-AL stock (now and change per year), the PSD (now, threshold, change per year and the years until the threshold is crossed, as a class), a breakdown per application and harvest, and warnings when harvest or phosphate content data are missing. `calculatePhosphateBalance` now takes `fdm`, is async, caches each field with `getPhosphateBalanceField` and reports failing fields in `fields[].errorMessage` (`hasErrors`, `fieldErrorMessages`) instead of failing the farm. The NMI cultivations lookup also accepts BRP codes and phosphate contents sent as numeric strings. When the crop history of a farm cannot be added, only that farm is marked as failed (the farm page shows its error state, the organisation page the failed farm) instead of the whole page failing. A farm whose input could not be collected is shown as failed instead of "Geen percelen". The aim label only says that environment and yield ask for a different direction in the true conflict (PSD above the threshold, P-AL below 35), and the advice gives the time until P-AL falls below 35 itself. The default timeframe of `collectInputForPhosphateBalance` ends at the end of 31 December. The mean highest groundwater level per groundwater class now follows the GHG ranges of the Dutch groundwater class definitions (`ghgRangePerGroundwaterClass`): the middle of a closed range and the lower bound of an open-ended one; this corrects Gt Ic, IIc and IVc. The projection takes the sampling depth from the most recent analysis with a phosphorus value; earlier years use their own land use for the mineralisation, and a harvest of an earlier year whose crop has no catalogue details is left out and reported instead of failing the field. A delivered PSD is also used when the oxalate triplet is unusable, and `collectInputForPhosphateBalance` checks that the requested field belongs to the farm. The organisation page says that the farm verdict counts only judged hectares and shows how much of the area was judged; the field dashboard tile calls the PSD estimated only when the PSD itself comes from NMI BodemSchat. There are no soil-type defaults for Fe-ox and Al-ox anymore: `calculatePhosphateSaturation` takes only the soil analyses, keeps a delivered PSD without Fe-ox and Al-ox (without a PSC, so not projected), and otherwise reports the PSD as unknown. The app adds the NMI BodemSchat estimate (oxalate values, P-CaCl₂, PSD) as an undated analysis to fields without oxalate data. The laboratory names on the phosphate pages come from fdm-core, an unreachable aim reads "onhaalbaar bij de huidige balans", the leaching is labelled as a first estimate that is still being developed, and the warning for a missing phosphate content no longer refers to the NMI API key. The user guide describes the phosphate balance in the chapter "Balansen".
