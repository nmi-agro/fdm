Add a prototype phosphate balance page ("Fosfaat") under Balans, for the farm and per field. The farm page shows the balance, supply and removal in kg P₂O₅/ha and the status of each field. The field page adds the P-AL stock (now and change per year), the PSD (now, threshold, change per year and the years until the threshold is crossed, as a class), a breakdown per application and harvest, and warnings when harvest or phosphate content data are missing. `calculatePhosphateBalance` now takes `fdm`, is async, caches each field with `getPhosphateBalanceField` and reports failing fields in `fields[].errorMessage` (`hasErrors`, `fieldErrorMessages`) instead of failing the farm. The NMI cultivations lookup also accepts BRP codes and phosphate contents sent as numeric strings. When the crop history of a farm cannot be added, only that farm is marked as failed (the farm page shows its error state, the organisation page the failed farm) instead of the whole page failing. A farm whose input could not be collected is shown as failed instead of "Geen percelen". The aim label only says that environment and yield ask for a different direction in the true conflict (PSD above the threshold, P-AL below 35), and the advice gives the time until P-AL falls below 35 itself. The default timeframe of `collectInputForPhosphateBalance` ends at the end of 31 December. The user guide describes the phosphate balance in the chapter "Balansen".
