Add organic-P mineralisation, deposition and leaching to the phosphate balance. Mineralisation is added to the supply with two tracks: on mineral soils from the soil organic matter degradation of the organic matter balance, on peat (`veen`) from peat oxidation by groundwater class (the organic matter degradation is not used there). Deposition adds 0.7 kg P₂O₅/ha/year. Leaching is estimated from the surplus only (surface runoff and erosion are not included) (fraction by the phosphorus saturation degree relative to the field threshold, times a groundwater factor), reported in `leaching` and, like nitrate leaching, not subtracted from the balance; the projection of P-AL and PSD does include it. Peat fields whose groundwater class changed get a warning that Fe-bound phosphate can be released on rewetting (not calculated). The phosphate pages show the leaching as a separate card and mineralisation and deposition in the breakdown. All parameters are drafts to be reviewed.

The phosphate balance chart now shows the terms as stacked bars like the nitrogen balance (deposition, mineralisation and fertilizers per type, with each application on field level, against harvests and crop residues), and the breakdown lists the fertilizer applications under their fertilizer type. `supply.fertilizers` now holds `{ total, applications }` per fertilizer type, and the farm result has the area-weighted `terms`.

The P-AL chart on the field page shows dated laboratory measurements of P-AL as points (NMI estimates are not shown as measurements). The saldo cards show the average balance of the last three years when each of the three years has fertilizer applications or harvests. The PSD card shows the soil-type note on its reliability; for dalgrond (former peat) and moerige klei it now says the PSD is probably underestimated (organically bound Fe and Al in the oxalate extraction, least certain map estimates). `PhosphateSaturation` has a new `soil_note`.

Add the organisation page of the phosphate balance ("Fosfaat" under Balans for organisations, in the header dropdown and the sidebar): the area-weighted saldo, supply, removal and leaching of the selected farms, the stacked chart, and per farm the saldo, the difference from the organisation average and the share of the area whose balance fits the PSD aim (✓ from 50%). `collectInputForPhosphateBalanceForFarms` collects the input of several farms with the catalogues fetched once, and `collectInputForPhosphateBalance` now uses the farm-level queries for a whole farm. The field overview gets a phosphate balance tile (saldo, status, PSD and threshold, years class, measured or estimated).
