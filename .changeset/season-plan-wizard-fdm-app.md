Add the season planning wizard "Plan seizoen" (first part). It opens from a new card on the farm dashboard and an item in the sidebar, and is always accessible. The wizard picks the year to plan (the current year while it is incomplete, else the next year), shows the progress of its seven steps derived from the recorded data, and continues at the first step that is not done. The start step lets the user switch between the current and the next year and shows per field whether it is kept, new or no longer available, its previous crops, the year the proposal is based on and the proposed crop. The other steps link to the existing pages until they get their own page.
