Add the season planning wizard "Plan volgend seizoen". It opens from a new card on the farm dashboard and an item in the sidebar, and is always accessible. The wizard picks the year to plan (the current year while it is incomplete, else the next year), shows the progress of its seven steps derived from the recorded data, and continues at the first step that is not done. The start step lets the user switch between the current and the next year and shows per field whether it is kept, new or no longer available, its previous crops, the year the proposal is based on and the proposed crop. In the Percelen step a field is kept by default or can be marked as no longer available (it then ends on 31 December of the year before and its ongoing measures end too); new fields can be added for the planned year. In the Gewassen step the main cultivation of each field is chosen, prefilled from the crop rotation or the previous year, with a choice of source year per field; continuing cultivations such as grassland can be ended and replaced. In the Vanggewas/groenbemester step a catch crop or green manure can be added after the main cultivation of each arable field, also where it is not mandatory; on sand and loess the expected reduction of the next year's nitrogen usage norm (article 28d Urm) is shown. In the Maatregelen step measures that continue are listed, measures that ended in the year before can be renewed (ongoing or up to the end of the planned year), and measures of fields that are no longer available are shown read-only. In the Bemesting step the fertilizer applications of an earlier year are copied per field (from the same field when the crop is unchanged, otherwise from the most recent field with the same crop, with the dates moved to the planned year); fields without such an example keep an empty plan and show the remaining nutrient advice. The filling of the farm's legal norms is shown live, marked as provisional when the norms of an earlier year are used, and a fertilizer plan can also be made with Gerrit. In the Oogst & overzicht step missing harvests are added (the harvests of the year before for continuing cultivations such as grassland, otherwise the standard yield of the cultivation catalogue) and can be adjusted on the harvest page; the overview shows the progress of every step, the filling of the legal norms, the nitrogen balance compared to the year before, the points that need attention (such as measures that lapse) and a link to the bemestingsplan PDF. New fields added on the map are numbered after the highest "Perceel N" of the farm in any year, so they no longer get the name of a field that is not in use anymore. For a year planned ahead that is not yet in the cultivation catalogue, the crop lists use the newest catalogue year, so all crops can be chosen. When Gerrit is opened from the wizard, applying its plan returns to the wizard. While the wizard is open, the calendar of the sidebar follows the planned year. The user manual has a new chapter "Plan volgend seizoen", and the sidebar, dashboard, Gerrit and bemestingsplan pages of the manual describe the wizard and that Gerrit works for 2025 and later.
