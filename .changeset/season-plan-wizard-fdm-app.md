Add the season planning wizard "Plan volgend seizoen". It opens from a new card on the farm dashboard and an item in the sidebar, and is always accessible. The wizard picks the year to plan (the current year while it is incomplete, else the next year), shows the progress of its seven steps derived from the recorded data, and continues at the first step that is not done. The start step lets the user switch between the current and the next year and shows per field whether it is kept, new or no longer available, its previous crops, the year the proposal is based on and the proposed crop. In the Percelen step a field is kept by default or can be marked as no longer available (it then ends on 31 December of the year before and its ongoing measures end too); new fields can be added for the planned year. In the Gewassen step the main cultivation of each field is chosen, prefilled from the crop rotation or the previous year, with a choice of source year per field; continuing cultivations such as grassland can be ended and replaced. The remaining steps link to the existing pages until they get their own page. New fields added on the map are numbered after the highest "Perceel N" of the farm in any year, so they no longer get the name of a field that is not in use anymore.
